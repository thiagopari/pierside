How it works
Five steps, one conversation

Pierre handles the conversation. Money moves only through verified code. No one can discount a job, invent a price, or book an unpaid slot.

1

Describe the site

Tell Pierre what you need inspected — pilings, seawall, hull, or moorings. Send a photo if you have one; Pierre reads it for growth and clarity.

→
2

Get a firm quote

Pierre asks only the missing questions, then calls the rate card. Every line item, the total, and the 30% deposit appear on your job sheet.

→
3

Pick a dive slot

Open slots with marine weather forecasts. Pierre recommends calm days and warns about rough conditions so dives don't get cancelled.

→
4

Pay the deposit

30% deposit through PayPal. The server captures, verifies to the cent, and books the slot. The 70% balance is invoiced after the dive.

→
5

Booked + pre-dive plan

Booking confirmed, cancellation policy shown, and Pierre writes a pre-dive plan: access, tide window, dive sequence, safety, deliverables.

Features
Built for real operations

Everything a dive contractor needs to go from inquiry to invoice, without the back-and-forth.

🤖

AI scoping with Pierre

Multi-turn conversation powered by Claude. Pierre asks follow-up questions, reads site photos for marine growth and water clarity, and never guesses a price.

💳

PayPal payments

Orders v2 for the deposit, Invoicing v2 for the balance. Verified webhooks handle closed tabs and refunds. The slot books only after the money lands.

🌊

Marine weather forecasts

NOAA forecast on every slot. Pierre recommends calm days and warns about wind and waves, reducing last-minute cancellations and wasted crew trips.

🔒

Tamper-proof pricing

The AI talks; a deterministic rate card prices. A prompt-injected or confused model cannot discount a job, change a price, or book without payment.

📋

Operator dashboard

Every booking in a sortable, filterable grid. Mark dives complete, record findings and hours, and PayPal emails the balance invoice to the customer.

🔍

Job history search

Elasticsearch hybrid search finds similar past inspections. Shows the customer what the crew found last time and helps operators estimate real dive hours.
